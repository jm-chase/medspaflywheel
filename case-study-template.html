The Challenge

When Glow MedSpa approached us in early 2024, they were facing a crisis that's all too common in the aesthetic industry. Despite offering premium Botox, fillers, and laser treatments in one of Miami's most affluent neighborhoods, they were struggling to consistently fill their appointment calendar.

Dr. Sarah Chen, the founder, had tried everything—Instagram ads, Facebook posts, even local print advertising. Nothing was delivering the steady stream of qualified leads she needed to keep her two treatment rooms busy. She was averaging just 14 new client appointments per month, well below the 40+ she needed to reach profitability and justify her overhead costs.

The situation was becoming unsustainable. With rent, equipment leases, and staff salaries to cover, Glow MedSpa needed a solution—fast. The marketing dollars she was spending weren't converting into bookings, and she was beginning to question whether her business model was viable at all.

Specific Pain Points:

  • Low-quality leads: Previous Facebook ads generated inquiries from tire-kickers and price shoppers, not serious buyers
  • High cost per acquisition: Paying $87 per lead with a 25% booking rate meant $348 to acquire one new client
  • Inconsistent results: Some months would bring 8 appointments, others 20—impossible to plan staff schedules or inventory
  • No clear attribution: Dr. Chen had no idea which marketing efforts were working and which were wasting money
  • Limited local visibility: Despite being in a prime location, they weren't showing up when potential clients searched for treatments nearby

Our Solution

After conducting a comprehensive audit of Glow MedSpa's market, competitors, and previous marketing efforts, we identified the core issue: they were fishing in the wrong pond. Social media ads were casting a wide net and attracting browsers, not buyers.

We pivoted to a high-intent Google Ads strategy, targeting people actively searching for specific treatments in Miami. This wasn't about brand awareness—it was about capturing demand that already existed and directing it to Glow MedSpa's calendar.

1

High-Intent Keyword Targeting

Built Google Ads campaigns around bottom-of-funnel keywords like "Botox Miami Beach," "lip filler near me," and "laser hair removal Coral Gables." These searchers weren't browsing—they were ready to book.

2

Geo-Targeted Local Service Ads

Implemented Google Local Service Ads with a 5-mile radius around the clinic, ensuring Glow MedSpa appeared at the very top of search results with verified credibility badges.

3

Landing Page Optimization

Created service-specific landing pages with clear pricing, before/after galleries, and prominent booking CTAs. Reduced friction by integrating direct calendar scheduling instead of contact forms.

4

Call Tracking & Attribution

Implemented call tracking and conversion pixels to measure every lead source, giving Dr. Chen complete visibility into which campaigns were driving actual revenue.
