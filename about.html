About MedSpaFlywheel

We're a team of marketing experts obsessed with helping medical spas grow. Our mission is simple: bring you more clients, more revenue, and measurable results—without the guesswork.

Our Story

MedSpaFlywheel was born from a simple observation: most medical spas are phenomenal at what they do—transforming clients' confidence and well-being—but struggle with the marketing side of their business. They're experts in aesthetics, not advertising. That's where we come in.

After years of working with aesthetic practices across the Southeast, we noticed a pattern. Generic marketing agencies don't understand the unique challenges of med spas: strict advertising regulations, high-ticket service sales cycles, reputation management complexities, and the need for HIPAA-compliant systems. We decided to build something different—an agency that speaks your language and understands your business inside and out.

Today, we're proud to partner with dozens of med spas, helping them fill their calendars with qualified clients, boost their average transaction values, and scale sustainably. Our approach combines cutting-edge digital marketing tactics with deep industry knowledge, ensuring every campaign we run is compliant, effective, and ROI-positive.

Why Med Spas?

We Speak Your Language

We know the difference between Botox and Dysport. We understand why clients research for months before booking. We're familiar with state regulations, before/after photo guidelines, and the importance of building trust before selling. This industry knowledge makes all the difference in crafting campaigns that resonate and convert.

We Understand Your Client Journey

Your potential clients aren't impulse buyers—they're making considered, often emotional decisions about their appearance and confidence. We design marketing funnels that nurture these prospects over weeks or months, providing education, building trust, and guiding them toward their first consultation.

We Solve Industry-Specific Challenges

From navigating Google's strict medical advertising policies to managing online reviews in a sensitive industry, we've tackled every challenge med spas face. Our systems are compliant, ethical, and designed specifically for the unique needs of aesthetic practices.
