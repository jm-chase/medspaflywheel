Our Services

Comprehensive marketing solutions designed specifically for medical spas. Everything you need to attract, convert, and retain more clients—all in one place.

Local Paid Advertising

What It Is

Strategic Google Local Service Ads (LSAs), Google Search campaigns, and Facebook/Instagram advertising designed to put your med spa in front of high-intent local clients actively searching for aesthetic treatments. We target specific demographics, locations, and treatment interests to maximize your advertising ROI.

Why It Matters

  • Immediate visibility: Appear at the top of Google search results when potential clients search for treatments you offer
  • High-intent traffic: Capture clients who are ready to book, not just browsing
  • Competitive advantage: Outrank competitors and capture market share in your service area
  • Measurable ROI: Track every dollar spent and see exactly which ads drive bookings

How We Do It

1

Market Research & Strategy

We analyze your local market, identify high-value keywords, and research competitor positioning to create a data-driven advertising strategy.

2

Campaign Setup & Launch

We create compelling ad copy, design eye-catching visuals, set up conversion tracking, and launch campaigns across Google and Facebook/Instagram.

3

Continuous Optimization

Daily monitoring, A/B testing, bid adjustments, and audience refinement to improve performance and reduce cost per acquisition.

4

Reporting & Scaling

Monthly performance reports with actionable insights. As ROI improves, we strategically scale campaigns to drive even more growth.

Results You Can Expect

Within the first 90 days, most clients see a 3-5x return on ad spend, with consistent month-over-month growth. Expect 20-50+ high-quality leads per month, depending on your market and budget.

Typical metrics: 2-4% click-through rate, $50-150 cost per qualified lead, 20-30% lead-to-appointment conversion rate.

Email Drip Campaigns

What It Is

Automated email sequences that nurture leads from initial inquiry to booked appointment. We create personalized messaging flows based on treatment interest, client stage, and behavior triggers—all running automatically in the background while you focus on delivering treatments.

Why It Matters

  • Convert more leads: 60-80% of leads don't book immediately—automated follow-up converts them over time
  • Save time: No more manual follow-ups or forgetting to check in with leads
  • Build relationships: Stay top-of-mind with valuable content, education, and special offers
  • Increase lifetime value: Re-engage past clients with reminders and new treatment announcements

How We Do It

1

Sequence Design & Copywriting

We map out email flows for different client journeys (new lead, consultation booked, post-treatment, etc.) and write compelling, conversion-focused copy.

2

Segmentation & Personalization

We segment your list by treatment interest, stage in journey, and demographics to deliver hyper-relevant messages that resonate.

3

Automation Setup

We integrate with your CRM or booking system and set up triggers so emails send automatically based on client actions.

4

Testing & Optimization

We A/B test subject lines, content, and send times to maximize open rates, click rates, and conversions.

Results You Can Expect

Expect 15-25% increase in lead-to-appointment conversion rates within 60 days. Email nurture campaigns typically achieve 30-40% open rates and 8-15% click-through rates—well above industry averages.

Clients also see reduced no-shows and increased repeat bookings from automated reminder and re-engagement campaigns.

How We Do It

1

Content Strategy & Calendar

Content Creation

Review Generation

What It Is

An automated system that systematically collects 5-star reviews from your happy clients across Google, Facebook, and other platforms. We make it easy for satisfied clients to leave reviews while monitoring and managing your online reputation.

Why It Matters

  • Dominate local search: 5-star ratings are the #1 factor in local SEO rankings
  • Build trust instantly: 93% of consumers read reviews before choosing a local business
  • Outshine competitors: More reviews and higher ratings = more clients choosing you over others
  • Protect your reputation: Proactive review generation dilutes the impact of occasional negative reviews

How We Do It

1

Automated Review Requests

After each appointment, clients automatically receive a friendly SMS or email requesting a review—timed perfectly when their experience is fresh.

2

Smart Routing

Happy clients are directed to Google/Facebook. Unhappy clients are routed to a private feedback form so issues can be resolved before a negative review goes public.

3

Reputation Monitoring

We monitor all reviews across platforms and alert you immediately to new reviews—both positive and negative—so you can respond promptly.

4

Response Management

We draft professional responses to reviews (you approve before posting) to show prospects you care about client satisfaction.

Results You Can Expect

Expect 10-20 new 5-star reviews per month, with 30-40% of clients leaving a review when asked (vs. 2-5% without automation). Your overall star rating typically increases by 0.3-0.5 stars within 90 days.

The compounding effect is powerful: more reviews = better local rankings = more visibility = more bookings.

Includes setup & monitoring
