Our Proven Process

A systematic, data-driven approach to growing your med spa. No guesswork, no surprises—just consistent, measurable results.

Six Steps to Sustainable Growth

Our proven framework takes you from strategy to scale in a clear, methodical way.

1

Discovery & Audit

We start by analyzing your current marketing efforts, competitive landscape, and market opportunities. Through in-depth discovery calls and a comprehensive audit, we identify what's working, what's not, and where the biggest opportunities lie.

Competitive Analysis Market Research Website Audit
2

Strategy Development

Based on our findings, we craft a custom marketing plan tailored specifically to your market, services, and growth goals. This isn't a cookie-cutter template—it's a strategic roadmap designed for your unique situation.

Custom Roadmap Budget Allocation KPI Setting
3

Implementation & Setup

Now comes the build phase. We set up your ad campaigns, automation systems, tracking pixels, and analytics dashboards. Everything is configured for compliance, performance, and measurability before we flip the switch.

Ad Account Setup Email Automation Tracking & Analytics
4

Launch & Optimize

Your campaigns go live, and we monitor performance closely. The first 30 days are crucial—we analyze data daily, making real-time adjustments to maximize performance and minimize wasted spend. This is where the magic starts to happen.

Campaign Launch A/B Testing Daily Monitoring
5

Scale & Grow

Once we've validated what works, we scale it strategically. We expand winning campaigns, test new markets and services, and increase budgets intelligently. Growth is intentional and sustainable—not reckless.

Budget Scaling New Channels Geographic Expansion
6

Report & Refine

Transparency is non-negotiable. You receive detailed monthly reports showing exactly what we've done, what results we've achieved, and what we're planning next. We meet regularly to review performance and refine strategy based on data and your evolving goals.

Monthly Reports Strategy Calls Continuous Improvement
