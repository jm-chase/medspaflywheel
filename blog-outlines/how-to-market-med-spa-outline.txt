- Age: 35-65 years old
- Gender: 70% female, 30% male

**Psychographics:**
- Values: Results, professionalism, safety, convenience

- Worried about safety and credentials

1. Survey your existing clients - what brought them in?
2. Check Google reviews of competitors - what do clients mention?
3. Join local Facebook groups - what questions do people ask?
4. Review your booking data - which services convert best?

### Section 2: Google Ads for Immediate Leads (600 words)

**H2:** Strategy #1: Google Ads for Immediate Med Spa Leads

**H3:** Why Google Ads Works

Google Ads captures high-intent searches from people actively looking for treatments **right now**.

When someone searches "botox near me" or "lip filler in [your city]," they're not researching—they're ready to book.

**Typical Results:**
- ROI: $4-6 returned per $1 spent
- Timeline: First leads within 48 hours
- Cost per lead: $25-60 depending on market
- Conversion rate: 15-25% of leads book consultations

**H3:** Campaign Structure

**Campaign 1: Brand Protection**
- Keywords: Your med spa name + variations
- Budget: $5-10/day
- Purpose: Prevent competitors from stealing your branded traffic
- Example: "MedSpa Flywheel", "MedSpa Flywheel Atlanta"

**Campaign 2: High-Intent Services**
- Keywords: "botox [city]", "lip filler near me", "coolsculpting [city]"
- Budget: $50-100/day
- Purpose: Capture ready-to-book clients
- Match type: Phrase and exact match

**Campaign 3: Informational Searches**
- Keywords: "how much does botox cost", "best med spa in [city]"
- Budget: $20-40/day
- Purpose: Capture early-stage researchers
- Match type: Broad match modified

**H3:** Ad Copy Best Practices

1. **Include pricing:** "Botox starting at $11/unit" filters unqualified leads
2. **Highlight offers:** "New client special" or "20% off first treatment"
3. **Show credentials:** "Board-certified providers"
4. **Add urgency:** "Limited appointments available"
5. **Use extensions:** Call buttons, location, sitelinks

**Example Ad:**
```
Headline: Botox in [City] | $11/Unit | Board-Certified
Description: New client special: 20% off first treatment. Same-day appointments available. 5-star rated. Book now!
```

**H3:** Landing Page Tips

- Botox ad → Botox landing page (NOT homepage)
- Include: Before/after photos, pricing, credentials, booking form
- Mobile-optimized (60% of searches are mobile)
- Fast loading (under 3 seconds)

**H3:** Compliance Considerations

⚠️ **Important:**
- Include disclaimers on before/after images
- Don't guarantee results
- Follow FDA guidelines for treatment names
- Check your state's medical advertising laws

**H3:** Real Example

An Atlanta med spa was spending $3,000/month on Facebook ads with minimal results. We shifted to Google Ads:

- Month 1: 45 leads, 12 consultations, 8 new clients
- Month 2: 68 leads, 18 consultations, 13 new clients
- Month 3: 92 leads, 24 consultations, 19 new clients

Cost per acquisition dropped from $425 to $158 (210% increase in bookings).

### Section 3: Email Marketing & Automation (500 words)

**H2:** Strategy #2: Email Marketing That Books Appointments

**Why Email Works:**
- Highest ROI: $42 per $1 spent (DMA)
- You own the list (unlike social media)
- Nurtures leads over time
- Automated = hands-free

**H3:** 5 Essential Email Sequences

**1. Welcome Series (3 emails over 5 days)**
- Email 1: Thank you + what to expect
- Email 2: Your treatment options
- Email 3: Special offer for new clients

**2. Abandoned Consultation Follow-Up**
- Trigger: Booked consultation but didn't show
- Email 1 (same day): "We missed you today"
- Email 2 (3 days): Reschedule + remove booking fee
- Email 3 (7 days): Last chance offer

**3. Post-Treatment Nurture**
- Day 3: "How are you feeling?"
- Week 2: Care tips + product recommendations
- Month 1: "Ready for your next treatment?"

**4. Re-Engagement for Inactive Clients**
- Trigger: No visit in 6 months
- Email 1: "We miss you" + what's new
- Email 2: Exclusive comeback offer
- Email 3: Final chance (limited time)

**5. Seasonal Promotions**
- Mother's Day specials
- Summer body prep
- Holiday gift cards
- New Year rejuvenation

**H3:** Email Marketing Tools

Top choices for med spas:
- Mailchimp (easiest for beginners)
- Klaviyo (best for e-commerce/products)
- ActiveCampaign (best automation)
- **Cost:** $30-150/month depending on list size

**H3:** Best Practices

- Subject lines: 40-50 characters, include benefit
- Send time: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm best
- Frequency: 2-4 emails/month (don't overwhelm)
- Personalization: Use first name, past treatments
- Mobile-optimized: 50%+ open on mobile

**Example Results:**
A Nashville med spa added email automation and saw:
- 25% conversion rate on welcome series
- 35% re-booking rate from post-treatment emails
- $18,000 additional revenue in first 90 days

### Section 4: Social Media Strategy (400 words)

**H2:** Strategy #3: Social Media for Med Spas

**Best Platforms:**
1. **Instagram** (primary) - Visual, target demographic
2. **Facebook** (secondary) - Older audience, local targeting
3. **TikTok** (emerging) - Younger audience, viral potential

**H3:** Content Pillars

**1. Before/After (30% of content)**
- Show real results (with consent)
- Include disclaimers
- Rotate different treatments
- Post 2-3x per week

**2. Educational (40% of content)**
- How treatments work
- What to expect
- Downtime and recovery
- Common questions answered

**3. Behind-the-Scenes (20% of content)**
- Meet the team
- Office tours
- Product spotlights
- Day in the life

**4. Client Testimonials (10% of content)**
- Video reviews
- Written testimonials
- Before/after with story
- Tag clients (with permission)

**H3:** Posting Strategy

- Frequency: 3-5 posts per week
- Best times: 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm
- Use Reels/Stories: Higher engagement
- Hashtags: 10-15 relevant, local tags

**Example Hashtags:**
```
#MedSpa #AtlantaMedSpa #Botox #LipFiller #AtlantaAesthetic
#SkinCare #AntiAging #AtlantaBeauty #GeorgiaMedSpa #BuckheadBeauty
```

**H3:** Compliance Notes

⚠️ Follow platform rules + medical advertising laws:
- Disclaimers on before/after
- Don't guarantee results
- Tag sponsored content
- Privacy: Never post without consent

### Section 5: Review Generation (300 words)

**H2:** Strategy #4: Building a 5-Star Reputation

**Why Reviews Matter:**
- 90% check reviews before booking (BrightLocal)
- Need 40+ reviews to be trusted
- Higher rankings in Google Maps
- Social proof = conversions

**H3:** Review Platform Priority

1. **Google** (most important)
2. **Yelp**
3. **RealSelf**
4. **Facebook**

**H3:** How to Ask for Reviews

**Timing:**
- Ask 3-7 days after treatment
- When client is happiest with results
- NOT right after (may be swollen/bruised)

**Method:**
- Text message with direct link (easiest)
- Email follow-up sequence
- In-person at checkout (awkward but effective)

**Template:**
```
Hi [Name]! We hope you're loving your [treatment] results!
Would you mind sharing your experience? Your feedback helps
us and other clients. Leave a review: [link]

Thank you! - [Your Med Spa]
```

**H3:** Responding to Reviews

**Positive reviews:**
- Thank them by name
- Mention their treatment
- Invite them back

**Negative reviews:**
- Respond within 24 hours
- Apologize and show empathy
- Offer to resolve offline
- Never argue publicly

**Example Response:**
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We're sorry your
experience didn't meet expectations. We'd like to make
this right. Please contact us at [phone] so we can
discuss this further.
```
