Why Niche-Specific Marketing Matters

Generic "med spa marketing" misses the nuances that drive results for your specific clinic type.

Different Buying Cycles

A Botox client rebooks every 3-4 months. An IV therapy client books on impulse. A peptide therapy patient researches for weeks. Each demands a different funnel.

Different Audiences

Demographics, platform preferences, and messaging that resonates vary dramatically between clinic types. Cookie-cutter ads underperform niche-targeted campaigns.

Different Regulations

Advertising restrictions vary by treatment type. Peptide and IV therapy clinics face platform-specific ad policies that require compliance-aware creative strategies.
